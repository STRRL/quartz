# Sazabi Manifesto - Logs Are All You Need

- Link: https://www.sazabi.com/manifesto
- Company: Sazabi Inc. (AI-native observability startup)
- Status: Read (page is JS-rendered, limited content available)

## Takeaway

- Core claim: logs alone can reconstruct metrics and traces — three pillars for the price of one
- AI makes this viable now: LLMs can extract structure, detect anomalies, and derive metrics from raw logs automatically
- "Monitoring is dead, agentic anomaly detection is the future"
- Basically the same thesis as Wide Events (ref 06) and O11y 2.0 (ref 07), but packaged as a product pitch
- Registered "LOGS ARE ALL YOU NEED" as a trademark
- For LAPP: validates the direction — if a funded startup is betting on "just logs + AI", the approach has legs
