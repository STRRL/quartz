Introduction to Course 1: Foundations fo User Experience Design

UX designers help make technology easier to **understand** and more **enjoyable** to use.

The principles of user experience actually stretch back thousands of years, all the way to ancient China, where the practice of Feng Shui (风水) began: "how furniture is positioned in a room, could bring someone luck or improve their health and happiness."
