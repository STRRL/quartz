Why is user-centered design important?

Because the user buys and uses your product. In addition, it's also important to solve problems that **people actually experience**, rather than only trying to solve problems **you personally experience**.

---

There is no substitute for personally watching and listening to real people.

Focus on the user and all else will follow.

---

Four steps user-centered design:

- Understand
- Specify
- Design
- Evaluate

![](/assets/images/2022-09-20-16-13-46.png)

---

Iteration is the **KEY**. Iteration is **doing something again, by building on previous versions and making tweaks**.
