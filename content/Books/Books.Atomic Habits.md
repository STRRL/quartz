什么是习惯

Habits are, simply, reliable solutions to recurring problems in our environment

如何养成好习惯?

- make it
  - Obvious
  - Attractive
  - Easy
  - Satisfying

Make It Obvious

- Habit Scoreboard
- 先不加评判地把所有的事情记下来, 而后再为其加分 or 减分;
- Pointing and Calling: 当一个事情内心想做, 身体不想做(或者相反)时, 把它说出来; 这是一种 pointing and calling(指差确认 ); 说完就会倾向于多思考, 然后作出更正确的选择;
- 制定计划: 我将在[Time], 在[Location]做[Behavior].
- 最常见的 trigger 是 Time 和 Location, 而实施意图(implementation intention)将很好地利用他们.
- implementation intention 指: 当情况 X 发生时, 我会执行响应 Y.
- Habit Stacking: 在做[某个已有的习惯]后, 我将会做[新习惯]
- No behavior happens in isolation.
- identify a current habit you already do each day and then stack your new behavior on top. This is called habit stacking.

---

Make It Attractive

- Habits are **dopamine-driven** feedback loop.
- **Reward anticipation** push the habit, not the fulfillment. More anticipation, more dopamine.
- Habit Stacking + Temptation Bundling:
  - After "Current Habit", I will "Habit I Need"
  - After "Habit I Need", I will "Habit I Want"
- The culture we live in determine which behavior are attractive to us.
- We tend to imitate the habits from 3 social groups:
  - The Close (Family and Friends)
  - The Many (Tribe and Community)
  - The Powerful (Who with status and prestige)
- The behavior which could get us **approval**, **respect** and **praise** is attractive to us.
